Player snapshot

Calm under pressure

Alli Schroder is a Canadian right-handed pitcher, captain of the Boston Hunters, and the fifth overall selection in the inaugural Women’s Pro Baseball League draft.

At 16, Schroder pitched the final 4 1/3 innings of Canada’s extra-inning win over the United States in the 2018 Women’s Baseball World Cup bronze-medal game. She returned to help Canada win another World Cup bronze medal in 2024.

Her path also includes becoming the first woman to sign and play in the Canadian Collegiate Baseball Conference and appearing as one of seven principal players in Jean Fruth’s documentary See Her Be Her.

Away from baseball, Schroder is a wildland firefighter and crew leader in British Columbia. Boston selected her fifth overall and named her the Hunters’ first captain—a new opportunity to lead and help shape a franchise from the ground up.
