Player snapshot

Making history her way

Denae Benites is an American catcher whose career connects the United States women’s national team, Banana Ball, and the inaugural Women’s Pro Baseball League season.

Benites represented the United States in 2019, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2026. The team won the 2019 COPABE Women’s Pan-American Championship and earned silver at the 2024 Women’s Baseball World Cup.

New York selected her sixth overall in the inaugural WPBL draft. She was named the Heights’ captain and hit the first home run in league history on opening day, August 1, 2026.

Published reporting also traces her path through varsity baseball in Las Vegas, EMT and volunteer-firefighter work in Delaware, and the Loco Beach Coconuts in Banana Ball.
