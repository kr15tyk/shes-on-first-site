Player snapshot

A career across inaugural moments

Claire Eccles is a Canadian baseball player whose career connects international competition, a barrier-breaking West Coast League appearance, and the inaugural Women’s Pro Baseball League season.

New York selected Eccles 38th overall in the inaugural WPBL draft. The league lists her as a center fielder, while its 2026 statistics show appearances at the plate and as a pitcher.

In June 2017, Eccles became the first woman to play in the collegiate West Coast League when she pitched in relief for the Victoria HarbourCats. She worked two innings against the Wenatchee AppleSox, allowing one hit, one walk, and two runs.

That appearance followed international competition with Canada and collegiate softball at the University of British Columbia. Nine years later, she joined another inaugural chapter in women’s baseball as a member of the New York Heights.
