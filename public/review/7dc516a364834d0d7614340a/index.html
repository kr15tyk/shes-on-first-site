Player snapshot

Always finding a place to play

Kelsie Whitmore is an American pitcher and outfielder, captain of the San Francisco Firebells, and the first overall selection in the inaugural Women’s Pro Baseball League draft.

Her baseball path spans eight documented United States national-team seasons, independent professional baseball, Banana Ball, and now the WPBL. She is also one of the seven principal players followed in Jean Fruth’s documentary See Her Be Her.

Whitmore has spent more than a decade finding places to keep playing baseball. Her professional stops include the Sonoma Stompers, Staten Island FerryHawks, Oakland Ballers, and Savannah Bananas—environments where women rarely had a roster path.

The WPBL gives that journey a different setting: a professional league built for women. San Francisco selected Whitmore first overall, named her captain, and has used her as both a hitter and starting pitcher.
