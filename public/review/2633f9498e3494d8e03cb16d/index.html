Player snapshot

A path that kept finding baseball

Ashton Lansdell is an American infielder and captain of the Los Angeles Queens whose career connects youth and college baseball, Division I softball, six United States national-team appearances, Banana Ball, and the inaugural Women’s Pro Baseball League season.

Los Angeles selected Lansdell in the first round with the seventh overall pick in the inaugural WPBL draft. The league lists her as its No. 1 third baseman, while USA Baseball’s 2026 roster lists her as an infielder/outfielder.

The Atlanta Journal-Constitution reported that she began playing baseball at age four. She played at Wheeler High School, made Team USA at 17, and continued into Georgia Highlands College. Ole Miss describes her as the first woman to play baseball at the NJCAA level.

After an ACL injury and the COVID interruption, she moved to Division I softball at FIU and Ole Miss while continuing her national-team baseball career.
